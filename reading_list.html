Reading List

This is an area of other videos/essays and such about topics that relate to design and narrative. These do not have a main focus or point that they build to, but instead help form opinions from as you continue to apply the ideas to other areas. You do not have to necessarily agree about the theses of the videos, but they serve as pieces to get started thinking about your own experiences and opinions. It's hard to recount all the videos that I have watched on these ideas, so this list will grow as I encounter more. If there are suggestions, they can also be submitted on the about page.

Video Essays

  • Core-A Gaming| Why Fighting Games Are Hard (YouTube) | A breakdown of the execution barrier and why it matters for the philosophy of fundamentals.
  • Design Doc| "What Makes A Great Rival Boss" (YouTube) | On how boss fights in games can be designed to rival the player rather than just challenge the player and what that means
  • Masahiro Sakurai on Creating Games| "In-Game Cutscenes" (YouTube) | A video on how long breaks from the action to watch a scene play out interact with the gameplay, and depending on implementation.
